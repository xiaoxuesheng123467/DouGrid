# Third-Party Notices

## HansBug/pindou-color-data

DouGrid includes color-reference data from
[`HansBug/pindou-color-data`](https://github.com/HansBug/pindou-color-data),
revision `178dafbc9e77d3de556550dbd058270200129186` (data generated 2026-05-19).

Included upstream files:

- `mard-221-alfonse-doudou/colors.json` (exposed in the app as `mard-221`)
- `mard-291-github/colors.json` (exposed in the app as `mard-291`)
- `coco-291/colors.json` (exposed in the app as `coco-291`)
- `artkal-c-197-official/colors.json` (exposed in the app as `artkal-c-197`)

The JSON assets are retained with their upstream metadata, including original
source URLs and per-color source identifiers. The application-facing IDs are
stable aliases; the color records themselves are unmodified.
A copy of the license is also packaged at `assets/palettes/HANSBUG_LICENSE.txt`.

The Artkal C197 dataset documents that some special-material RGB values were
sampled from Artkal's official chart image because the official RGB PDF does
not publish numeric values for those colors. Its transparent CT entries use
RGBA preview values; alpha is not a claim about physical light transmission.

RGB values are screen references and can differ from physical beads. Confirm
color-critical work against a current physical color card.
